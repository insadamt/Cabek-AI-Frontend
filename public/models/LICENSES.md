# Scanner and vehicle assets

This file records the provenance and reuse requirements of the model inputs and
their rendered derivatives. The live category scene depends on Three.js and
the local Draco decoder. Blender's glTF importer remains available for offline
model and composition checks.

## Supplied sedan — active vehicle

`studio-v2/cabek-vehicle.glb` is derived from the user's supplied
`/home/insayd/Desktop/3D/model.blend`. No original author or license was included
with that file; no third-party license is inferred. The earlier Ferrari credit
does not describe this sedan.

The export retains the supplied sedan's geometry, interior and embedded PBR maps.
Project adaptations include a 4.8 metre scale, portable pearl paint and glazing,
1K embedded textures, four rolling wheel pivots, material batching and Draco mesh
compression. Reproduce with
[`export_user_vehicle.py`](../../scripts/blender_mcp/export_user_vehicle.py).
The original `.blend` is read without modification. Asset dimensions and export
details are recorded in [`vehicle-manifest.json`](studio-v2/vehicle-manifest.json).

## CABEK inspection tunnel

The active tunnel is an original Blender model of the photographed installation.
The CABEK panel lettering is reproduced as geometry. The runtime model uses
embedded materials. The source, export instructions and reference assumptions
are in the [studio v3 guide](../../scripts/scanner/README.md).

The editable v2 Blender studio packs `studiolights/world/studio.exr` from Blender
5.2's bundled studio lights. Its accompanying `license.txt` credits Greg Zaal /
Poly Haven and licenses all bundled HDRIs under CC0. This environment is used by
the offline Blender composition and its reference render; the frontend builds
its own reflection studio from geometry.

- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- [License inventory maintained by Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/issues/23089).

The previous live scene adapted the vehicle to pearl clearcoat paint, dark optical glass,
rubber tyres and alloy wheels/trim. Earlier cinematic derivatives used petrol paint.
These adaptations change lighting, camera,
placement and wheel animation, while retaining the original vehicle geometry.
The earlier prototype render used graphite paint. CABEK is not affiliated with
the car manufacturer.

## CABEK scanner

Custom procedural geometry authored for this project. Reproducible source:
[`scripts/scanner/build_scanner.py`](../../scripts/scanner/build_scanner.py).
Export: `cabek-scanner.glb`. The cinematic scenes adapt the scanner's graphite,
metal and emissive materials and combine it with authored photographic frames
and document geometry.

## Rendered derivatives

`scanner-preview.webp` is the retained offline render from the original
prototype. The films and posters under `public/cinematic/` are generated by
[`scripts/cinematic/`](../../scripts/cinematic/README.md). The Ferrari artist
attribution and CC BY 4.0 license apply to the vehicle in all these renders;
the retained film/model license files carry that attribution. The current page
uses the separately supplied sedan described above.

See [the cinematic asset attribution](../cinematic/LICENSES.md) for the notice
accompanying the current films and posters.
