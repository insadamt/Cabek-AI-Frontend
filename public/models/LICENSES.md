# Offline scanner and vehicle assets

This file records the provenance and reuse requirements of the model inputs and
their rendered derivatives. The models depend on Blender's glTF importer and
native Draco bridge for offline rendering; the category page displays local
films and posters without a browser 3D renderer or JavaScript decoder.

## Ferrari 458 Italia

- Artist: **vicent091036**.
- [Original model](https://sketchfab.com/models/57bf6cc56931426e87494f554df1dab6).
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- [Pinned model distribution in Three.js r180](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/models/gltf/ferrari.glb).
- [Attribution in the official example](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/webgl_materials_car.html).
- [License inventory maintained by Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/issues/23089).

The cinematic derivatives adapt the vehicle to deep petrol metallic paint,
dark optical glass, alloy wheels and trim. They change lighting, camera,
placement and wheel animation, while retaining the original vehicle geometry.
The earlier prototype render used graphite paint. CABEK is not affiliated with
the car manufacturer.

## CABEK scanner

Custom procedural geometry authored for this project. Reproducible source:
[`scripts/scanner/build_scanner.py`](../../scripts/scanner/build_scanner.py).
Export: `cabek-scanner.glb`. The cinematic scenes adapt the scanner's graphite,
metal and emissive materials and combine it with authored photographic frames
and document geometry.

## Rendered derivatives

`scanner-preview.webp` is the retained offline render from the original
prototype. The films and posters under `public/cinematic/` are generated by
[`scripts/cinematic/`](../../scripts/cinematic/README.md). The Ferrari artist
attribution and CC BY 4.0 license apply to the vehicle in all these renders;
the current page displays the credit and links to the artist and license.

See [the cinematic asset attribution](../cinematic/LICENSES.md) for the notice
accompanying the current films and posters.
