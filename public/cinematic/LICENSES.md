# Category film assets

This file records the source and attribution of the pre-rendered category films
and WebP posters. They depend on the offline Blender scene source and original
vehicle/tunnel geometry; they do not require a browser 3D renderer.

The Ferrari 458 Italia model is by **vicent091036**, under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- Original: https://sketchfab.com/models/57bf6cc56931426e87494f554df1dab6
- Pinned distribution: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/models/gltf/ferrari.glb
- Attribution source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/webgl_materials_car.html
- License inventory: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/issues/23089

The category films/posters adapt the vehicle's materials to petrol paint and
studio lighting, animate its placement, and combine it with the custom CABEK
tunnel, photographic frames and document geometry. The vehicle geometry remains
attributed to its artist. CABEK is not affiliated with the vehicle manufacturer.
The page displays artist and license credit.

The scanner and category scene source were authored for this project in
`scripts/scanner/` and `scripts/cinematic/`.
